Subject: Inventory accuracy — what I need for Friday's memo

Fatima,

Tobias and I need to give the auditors (and our bigger merchants) a credible answer on how trustworthy our stock numbers are, where it breaks down, and whether it's getting better or worse. This feeds the joint audit response and will likely shape where we invest in warehouse process fixes next quarter — so I'd rather have three sharp answers than ten partial ones. Please use your nightly slots on these, in rough priority order:

1. **How big is the gap between what we think we have and what's actually there?** Across our warehouses, how often do physical counts come back different from the stock we had on record, and how large are those differences in units (and ideally value)? I want a sense of scale before anything else.

2. **Where is it worst?** Does the mismatch concentrate in particular warehouses, or particular kinds of storage locations (e.g. bulk shelving vs. pick bins)? If a handful of sites or location types account for most of the problem, that tells us where to send help first.

3. **Is it a product or a process problem?** Are the discrepancies spread evenly across products, or concentrated in certain categories, high-velocity items, or items we handle in lots/serials? This will tell Tobias and me whether this is a handling issue or a tracking-method issue.

4. **Are our "soft holds" on stock reliable?** When we reserve stock for an order, does that reservation actually get fulfilled as expected, or do we see a lot of stuck/expired holds that quietly overstate what's available to sell? This matters for whether we're overpromising stock to customers.

5. **Is this getting better or worse over time?** A simple trend over the last several months would tell us whether recent process changes are helping or whether the problem is growing — useful context for the auditors either way.

If you can only get to three this week, do 1, 2 and 3 — those are the ones Tobias and I will lean on most heavily in the audit conversation. Happy to talk through trade-offs before you lock in Monday night's requests.
