1. **What does each merchant actually net us, after processing costs?** We only see the economics clearly once payouts are broken into gross, fee, and net — a merchant with huge volume but thin margin on our fee side could look better on revenue than it really is on contribution.

3. **Who's slow to pay us, and where?** For the B2B wholesalers especially, I'd like a sense of outstanding receivables and how long they're taking to settle — that's a cost of serving them that doesn't show up in the headline revenue.

If there's room, a rough read on how refund patterns differ by vertical or country would help round out the "which merchants are worth growing" question.
