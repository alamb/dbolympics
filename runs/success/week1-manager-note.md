1. **Who are our biggest and most important merchants, and how are they actually doing?** I want a sense of the size of each merchant's business with us (sales volume, order activity) and whether it's growing, flat, or shrinking lately. This is the backbone for every QBR conversation.

2. **Which merchants look like they're at risk right now?** I'm thinking about warning signs like slowing sales, rising refunds or cancellations, unpaid invoices, or a drop-off in shopper activity. I want a shortlist I can proactively reach out to before they churn.

3. **Which merchants are thriving, and is there a pattern?** I'd like to know who's growing fastest and whether there's anything they have in common (plan tier, vertical, region) that we could point to as a playbook for others.

4. **How healthy is each merchant's own customer base?** For our top merchants, I want to understand whether their shoppers are coming back and buying again, or whether they're mostly one-and-done. This tells me how defensible their business is, which matters for how I advise them.

5. **Are merchants getting good support from us, and does it affect how they feel about us?** I'd like a read on ticket volume and resolution experience for our top merchants, since I'll likely get asked about this directly in reviews.

For context: these go straight into prep notes for one-on-one merchant reviews, so I care most about being able to point to specific merchants by name and tell a clear story about each one's trajectory. Broad trends across all 120 merchants are useful background, but the top merchants are what I'll be sitting across the table from this month.
