Subject: Churn task force — what I'd like us to look at from Growth

Hi Arjun,

Glad this is getting cross-functional attention. From the marketing side, here's what I think matters:

1. **Do shoppers who stop engaging with our marketing touches (emails, campaigns, loyalty) churn sooner than the ones who stay engaged?** If disengagement shows up weeks before a churn, that's an early signal worth acting on.

2. **Does a merchant's own marketing activity level relate to whether their shoppers stick around?** I want to know if merchants who run fewer campaigns, promotions, or loyalty programs are the ones seeing higher shopper churn — that would tell us whether under-investment in marketing tools is part of the story.

3. **Is there a pattern in how promo- or discount-driven shoppers behave right before leaving** — e.g. do they go quiet after a coupon or loyalty redemption, or does spend drop off without any further marketing response from us?

Happy to jump on a call if useful — mainly want to make sure whatever "early warning" signals come out of this include the marketing-touch side, not just support or payment signals.
