Subject: CX signals for the churn task force

Arjun,

A few things I'd want the task force to look at from the shopper/support side:

1. **Does ticket experience precede merchant churn?** For merchants who later left, did their shoppers see slower first responses, more reopened tickets, or more escalations in the months before? I'd want to know how far out a warning shows up.

2. **Do returns/refunds friction and churn move together?** Where refunds get delayed, disputed, or partially handled, do we see both unhappy shoppers (low ratings/CSAT) and merchants churning shortly after? I'm curious whether refund delay is a leading indicator or just a symptom.

3. **Are complaint themes shifting before a merchant leaves?** Is there a recognizable pattern in what shoppers are complaining about (e.g. shipping, product quality, billing) in the weeks before a merchant churns, versus a stable merchant?

If we can pin down even one or two of these as reliable early signals, it changes what Samuel's team and I should be monitoring week to week rather than finding out after the fact.
