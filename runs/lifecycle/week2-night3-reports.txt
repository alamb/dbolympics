=== Report 3: Member order behavior before vs after their first points redemption (written by Bea) ===
Engineer's note: Population = users with a loyalty_accounts row and >=1 'burn' loyalty_transactions row; split is on their first burn's occurred_at. For exposure-after's end date, since the schema has no customer-level churn flag, I used the tenant's status: if the merchant tenant has churned, exposed-after runs to the customer's last order after redemption, otherwise it is right-censored at 2025-12-31 (i.e. "still active" = tenant still active on the platform). AOV uses non-cancelled orders converted to EUR via exchange_rates; refund_rate_pct and order counts use all orders (any status) in the period.
