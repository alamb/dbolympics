Ingrid, this is excellent — especially catching that the refund-rate gap was a vertical mix artifact. Good instinct to go back and break that out rather than let the scary headline stand.

What surprised me most, and what I want explained before we take this to Tobias as a joint recommendation: a 19.4% cost-to-member-revenue ratio is strikingly high, and members showing *lower* revenue and order counts than non-members is the opposite of what a loyalty programme is supposed to produce. I don't want to present "the programme loses money" to leadership until I understand whether that's a true causal effect or another mix artifact like the refund rate turned out to be — since 72% of members sit in one vertical, I'm wary of a second apples-to-oranges comparison hiding in there.

Questions for this week, in my own words:

1. **Within Apparel alone** — since that's where almost all members live — do members still look worse off than similar non-member customers in that same vertical, once we also account for how long they've been customers? That's the comparison I actually trust as a verdict on the programme.

2. **Is the growing points liability a timing issue or a real design flaw?** I want to understand whether we're simply in a "young programme, redemptions lag issuance" phase that will level out, or whether the redemption rate structurally understates true future cost — i.e., should I expect that €491k to keep climbing indefinitely at this pace.

3. **Can we fix the redemption-to-order linkage well enough to test the gaming hypothesis properly?** That was left untested, not cleared, and it's exactly the kind of finding that would change my recommendation, so I'd like one more serious attempt before we call it inconclusive for good.

4. **If the member economics do hold up as genuinely negative, is there a design change that would plausibly fix it** — rather than only "keep, cut, or extend to B2B" as the choice — are there any patterns in how the better-performing members (if any exist) use the programme differently from the rest? I don't need a redesign from you, just whether the data shows any sign of what a healthier version would look like.

The B2B extension question can wait — I agree we shouldn't open that door until we trust the member economics number. Let's keep that out of this week's slots unless there's room left over.
