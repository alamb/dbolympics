- Head of Growth Marketing team (Sofie Nilsen): Referrals are our most under-used strength: people who refer others order 1.5-2.4x more often than everyone else in every single product category, and over 40% start referring within their first 1-3 orders — earlier than we assumed, meaning we can likely prompt for referrals sooner.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Castell Fasteners' site traffic drop is a real warning sign, not normal B2B seasonality: it's down 10.6% year-over-year while 4 of its 5 B2B peers grew 11-86% over the same period.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Good news on churn: no established merchant (20+ lifetime orders) has gone silent for 180+ days - the only 9 merchants who've actually churned were all small, low-volume accounts that never gained traction.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Two popular 'early warning' theories for shopper churn didn't hold up: email open rates are nearly identical for shoppers about to go quiet vs. staying active (36% either way), and refunds process in under a day for virtually everyone, so there's no 'slow refund' group to test against satisfaction.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): The Feb 2025 login-failure spike wasn't random: 96.5% of it (7,168 of 7,429 failures) hit just 3 merchants on the same 4 days (Feb 8-11) — pattern points to a shared technical glitch, not a break-in, but our alerts stayed silent (0 flagged events) on every one of those 4 days.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): Data privacy requests check out end-to-end: all 60 requests we've ever received (48 since 2023, plus 12 older ones) are completed, and the 3 that looked unresolved for years were actually closed on time — just missing a timestamp.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): Flagged compliance/security incidents are rare and handled well: only 8 in 3 years, 100% resolved, averaging 1-2.5 weeks to close, none sitting open.
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 🔑 The checkout cliff is really a login wall: carts from shoppers who never log in convert at 0% (0 of ~8,900 in 2025), while logged-in shoppers convert at 51-68% — getting people to log in matters more than anything else in the cart.
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 📉 The cart drop-off is a long-standing structural pattern, not a recent problem: conversion held flat (~32-38%) every month in 2025, and a specific email-system bug in late November left no detectable dent — so it's not something that broke, it's something to redesign.
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 📧 We don't actually have an abandoned-cart reminder email today: what looked like a 'recovery nudge' last week turned out to be order/shipping confirmations, not a dedicated reminder — that's a real gap, and a real opportunity, to build.
