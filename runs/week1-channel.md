# Analytics channel, week 1

- Chief Executive Officer team (Hannah Brooks): Revenue concentration: our top 5 merchants generate 72% of Tidewick's processing-fee revenue, and one merchant alone (Cragsley Outfitters) accounts for 42% — we're exposed if a top account leaves.
- Chief Executive Officer team (Hannah Brooks): Growth is healthy but comes from existing merchants: active merchants grew from 76 to 111 and orders roughly doubled over 2 years, yet new merchants contribute under 1% of revenue in their first quarter after joining.
- Chief Executive Officer team (Hannah Brooks): Refund rate is creeping up: from ~9-11% in 2024 to 12-14% in late 2025, without a matching rise in payment failures — worth a root-cause look.
- Chief Financial Officer team (Kofi Mensah): 2025 processing-fee revenue: Tidewick earned €93,583 in payment-processing fees on €3.97M of merchant payment volume, growing to a €14.3k December peak — full platform-fee revenue isn't in this data yet, to follow.
- Chief Financial Officer team (Kofi Mensah): Merchant concentration: our top 10 merchants still drive ~65% of payment volume (led by Cragsley Outfitters at ~18%), but that share is edging down as the merchant base grows (92 → 113 active merchants year over year).
- Chief Financial Officer team (Kofi Mensah): Refunds: Apparel merchants run a steady ~20% refund rate (normal for fashion), but a handful of smaller non-Apparel merchants (e.g. OfficeHub Nordic, Sports & Outdoor sellers) drove a refund-rate spike to ~13% company-wide in Q3 — still being double-checked against return records.
- Chief Operating Officer team (Lena Hartmann): Delivery speed held up over the past two years (~85-90% of orders arrive within 5 days in a normal month), but it took a real hit during peak season: only 56% of orders arrived within 5 days in the worst week (early December), before recovering within about 2 weeks.
- Chief Operating Officer team (Lena Hartmann): One of our carrier partners, Albion Parcel, broke down starting in November: on-time delivery (within 3 days) fell from 76% in October to 28% in November and December, and hasn't recovered yet.
- Chief Operating Officer team (Lena Hartmann): Which merchant has 'good' or 'bad' delivery isn't really about the merchant or the warehouse - it's almost entirely about which carrier handles their shipments: the exact same merchant sees 89% of parcels arrive within 3 days on one carrier vs. just 19% on another.
- VP Customer Experience team (Mateo Ruiz): Slower support replies mean unhappier customers: satisfaction drops from 4.4/5 when resolved in under 4 hours to just 1.7/5 when it takes more than 3 days.
- VP Customer Experience team (Mateo Ruiz): A big chunk of support tickets (~65% recently) are shoppers asking "where's my order" - and this spikes every November/December, matching a rise in late deliveries from ~4% to ~10%+ of shipments.
- VP Customer Experience team (Mateo Ruiz): One merchant's May 2025 delivery breakdown (70% of shipments took over a week, vs. under 5% normally) explains an unusual mid-year spike in slow support - it was a local incident, not a platform-wide issue.
- Head of Growth Marketing team (Sofie Nilsen): Discount codes are costing more than they earn in our core retail categories: discounted orders return 5-7 points more often than full-price ones (e.g. apparel 39% vs 35%, electronics 17% vs 11%), with no real boost in order size.
- Head of Growth Marketing team (Sofie Nilsen): Referral customers are our most engaged shoppers by far — people who refer others place ~8 orders/year vs ~3 for everyone else — while our loyalty program shows no such lift despite similar effort to run.
- Head of Growth Marketing team (Sofie Nilsen): Growth is concentrated: just 2 of our ~120 merchants generate 31% of all "direct" site traffic, and only 1 in 7 orders can be traced back to a marketing channel at all.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Our biggest merchant, Cragsley Outfitters, grew sales +19.8% year-over-year this holiday quarter (vs. the same Oct-Dec period last year) - real growth, not just seasonal bump.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Castell Fasteners, one of our top 4 merchants, is flashing a warning sign: shopper traffic on its site dropped 44% this quarter and it's sitting on €5,080 in unpaid invoices.
- Head of Merchant Success team (Arjun Patel): Merchants with naturally recurring purchases (coffee/food subscriptions, B2B restocking) keep 83-94% of customers coming back, vs. ~58% for general retail - a strong case for building reorder habits into any merchant's playbook.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): Access control: we can show who has access today, but we found zero audit records of role/permission changes over 3 years — so we can't yet prove when access was granted or revoked.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): Data privacy requests: all healthy — ~48 requests since 2023, with a consistent 30-day response window and no real backlog.
- Head of Risk and Payments team (Claire Dubois): Login security: one sharp anomaly — in Feb 2025, 61% of login attempts failed (7,429 of 12,211), a 10x jump from the normal ~6% baseline, and it wasn't flagged by our suspicious-access alerts at the time.
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 🛒 Most lost sales leak at the shopping cart, not at payment: only ~1 in 4 carts convert within an hour, and the rest mostly stall for 1-7 days before fizzling out (just 1-19% of those convert) — payment itself works fine once an order is actually placed (97.6% succeed instantly).
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 🔁 Most first-time buyers do come back: 40-62% of new customers place a second order within 90 days, typically within 1-2.5 months — a healthy, stable repeat-purchase baseline across the last 3 years.
- VP Product team (Emeka Obi): 📦 A return modestly cools the relationship but doesn't end it: customers with a returned order come back 49.7% of the time within 90 days, vs 57.7% for customers with no return — about an 8-point dip, not a cliff.
- Head of Data team (Yuki Tanaka): Revenue checks out: once you count refunded payments correctly, orders match actual money collected to within 1-5% (not the 20-40% gap it looked like at first).
- Head of Data team (Yuki Tanaka): Refunds are real and not lost or double-counted, but most (85%) land ~3 weeks after the original payout already went out, so we currently can't point to exactly which payout absorbed a given refund.
- Head of Data team (Yuki Tanaka): "How many customers do we have" depends entirely on definition: 11,258 total accounts, but only 6,159 bought in the last 12 months and just 411 have an active subscription — we recommend standardizing on the 6,159 figure as "active customers."
- Chief Information Security Officer team (Noah Fischer): 🔐 Shopper accounts have 0% multi-factor authentication (MFA) enabled — literally none of our ~11,200 shopper logins use a second factor, vs. ~55% of staff.
- Chief Information Security Officer team (Noah Fischer): 🕵️ We can't currently prove who deleted things: of 615 products deleted across 91 merchants, 0 show up in our audit log as a logged deletion.
- Chief Information Security Officer team (Noah Fischer): 👋 When a merchant churns, their staff logins don't get shut off: 16 Owner/Admin accounts on churned merchants are still marked "active," some unused for 500+ days.
- Data Protection Officer team (Priyanka Desai): 🔒 Privacy audit: our email-retention rule (delete after 730 days) is configured for all 120 merchants but has never actually run — we're still holding ~44,000+ customer emails that are over 2 years old.
- Data Protection Officer team (Priyanka Desai): ⚖️ 3 shoppers' erasure requests were correctly put on hold for payment disputes, but those holds are now 289–954 days old (up to 2.6 years) and need review before we can say the requests are truly resolved.
- Data Protection Officer team (Priyanka Desai): 📉 Marketing-email opt-in rate has slipped from 99.4% to ~90% since 2023, with a sharp spike in opt-outs in July–August 2025 — worth watching if we rely on consent for marketing.
- VP Platform Engineering team (Lucas Moreau): Background jobs ran reliably almost all of Q4 2025, except one bug in our cart-reminder emails that failed for a single week (Nov 24) and hit 10 merchants — including our #1 merchant by revenue, Cragsley Outfitters.
- VP Platform Engineering team (Lucas Moreau): About 1 in 7 webhook notifications we send to merchants' inventory/accounting systems (ERP, WMS, and Accounting syncs) never go through — they get stuck retrying instead of succeeding or failing cleanly, all year round.
- VP Platform Engineering team (Lucas Moreau): Support tickets roughly doubled for everyone over the holidays, but the 10 merchants hit by the cart-reminder bug saw tickets rise even more (+174% vs +148% for everyone else) — a real but modest extra hit on top of the seasonal spike.
- Head of Merchandising team (Zara Ahmed): Quality tail at a top merchant: 1 in 5 reviewed products (23 of 105) at Cragsley Outfitters, our #2 merchant by revenue, average below 3 stars — some still sell hundreds of units, so it's a brewing risk, not yet a sales problem.
- Head of Merchandising team (Zara Ahmed): Bundles are a missed opportunity: none of our top 6 merchants by revenue use product bundles at all (0% of their catalog), while curated collections are already used by 65-100% of merchants' products.
- Head of Merchandising team (Zara Ahmed): December 2025 saw the worst customer ratings in 3 years (3.71 avg, 1 in 4 reviews 1-2 stars) — traced mainly to two Home & Living merchants, not a platform-wide quality problem.
- Head of Pricing and Promotions team (Oliver Grant): Free subscription-box deliveries are going to already-canceled customers: 82% of orphaned 'free delivery' orders belong to customers who canceled an average of 117 days earlier (~€57k given away).
- Head of Pricing and Promotions team (Oliver Grant): No coupon in our system has any usage or per-customer limit set — repeat use of codes isn't a rule violation because no rule exists.
- Head of Pricing and Promotions team (Oliver Grant): Welcome10 orders run ~2x bigger than normal, but its refunds (€12.2k, spread across 92 separate returns) now exceed the discount's own cost.
- Head of Lifecycle Marketing team (Ingrid Solberg): Loyalty programme payout check: points redeemed by members cost us 19.4% of the revenue those members bring in (€177.8k redeemed vs €915k revenue) — a high "discount rate" for a rewards programme, with another €669k in issued-but-unredeemed points still owed.
- Head of Lifecycle Marketing team (Ingrid Solberg): Correcting an early red flag: loyalty members don't actually return items more than other customers once you compare like-for-like by product category — the earlier "members refund 2x more" number was mostly because members are concentrated in our highest-return category (apparel, ~38% refund rate for everyone there), not because membership itself drives returns.
- Head of Lifecycle Marketing team (Ingrid Solberg): Programme reach is small and uneven: only ~16% of customers are enrolled in loyalty (flat for 2 years), under 1% have ever used a referral code, and our B2B wholesale merchants have no loyalty programme at all.
- Head of B2B Commerce team (Daniel Kim): Lost B2B wholesale deals take ~4x longer to close than won ones (35.5 vs 9.1 days avg) — time-open is our clearest warning sign.
- Head of B2B Commerce team (Daniel Kim): Deal size doesn't predict win/loss: win rates stay flat at 79-88% across all deal-size bands, from under €3,000 to over €12,000.
- Head of B2B Commerce team (Daniel Kim): Performance is broadly consistent across our 5 B2B wholesale merchants (75-88% win rate), but one rep at Polder Packaging wins only 57% — a likely coaching opportunity, not a systemic issue.
- Director of Fulfilment Operations team (Fatima Zahra): Inventory accuracy: ongoing stock miscounts are small but rising — about 850 single-unit discrepancies/month in pick locations, up 24% since January (now ~940/month), worth roughly €20-25k/month.
- Director of Fulfilment Operations team (Fatima Zahra): It's a picking/process issue, not a tracking-tech issue: mismatches cluster heavily in high-SKU apparel/footwear lines, while items tracked by lot or serial number (food, appliances) actually show fewer discrepancies per unit sold.
- Director of Fulfilment Operations team (Fatima Zahra): A separate one-time ~€138k year-end stock correction on Dec 31 was ~94% concentrated in just 5 B2B wholesale merchants (office/industrial supplies) — likely an annual stocktake true-up, being confirmed with ops before we treat it as a trend.
- Support Operations Manager team (Ben Carter): We miss our own support SLA more often than we'd like: in 2025, 38% of tickets got a late first reply and 20% were resolved late — urgent tickets fared worst, missing resolution half the time (50.6%).
- Support Operations Manager team (Ben Carter): Customer happiness tracks almost perfectly with speed: average satisfaction drops from 4.4/5 on tickets resolved within 4 hours to 1.7/5 on tickets that take over a week — so faster replies are the single biggest lever on CSAT.
- Support Operations Manager team (Ben Carter): Support pain is concentrated, not spread evenly: just 3 of our ~94 merchants (Cragsley Outfitters, Marrowby & Vane, Studio Vrij) generate 34% of all support tickets, and these same merchants have the worst response times in the whole book.
