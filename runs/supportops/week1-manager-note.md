Subject: Helpdesk operating review — what I need this week

Hi Ben,

Daniel and I need a baseline read on how the helpdesk actually runs, so we can walk into our first leadership review with a clear picture instead of impressions. This feeds our decisions on how we staff/structure agent groups, where we invest in macros and knowledge base content, and whether our current SLA targets are realistic. Given you only get a handful of reports a night, here's what matters most, roughly in priority order:

1. **Where is ticket volume concentrated, and are the right groups/agents carrying it?** I want to see how load splits across our queues and agent groups, and whether staffing looks balanced against that load — this feeds a near-term staffing/routing decision.

2. **Are we meeting our SLA promises, and where do we miss most?** I want to understand how often we breach first-response and resolution targets, and whether certain priorities, groups, or times are the repeat offenders. This is the headline number Daniel will want for the review.

3. **What actually makes a ticket slow?** I'd like a sense of what separates a ticket that resolves quickly from one that drags — whether that's certain topics, channels, or simply sitting unassigned — so we know where to intervene first.

4. **Are macros and knowledge base articles pulling their weight?** I want to know how much agents lean on canned replies and how the knowledge base is doing with customers (are articles helping people avoid a ticket, and are the ones we have actually rated as useful) — this feeds a content/investment decision for next quarter.

5. *(If there's room this week)* How satisfied are customers coming out of resolved tickets, and does that satisfaction track with the SLA and speed picture above, or is something else driving it?

No need to go deep on all five — pick what fits in the nightly slots and flag anything surprising even if I didn't ask directly. Keep it at a level Daniel and I can act on quickly; we'll dig deeper live once we see the numbers.
