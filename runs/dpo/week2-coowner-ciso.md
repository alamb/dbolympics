Hi Priyanka,

A few things from my side that would strengthen the privacy response:

1. When a data subject request results in erasure, can we show what login/session and access history remains afterward, and for how long — i.e. do our retention rules actually clear it out, or does it linger?

2. For consent withdrawals, do we have any evidence that we stop using/collecting the related data promptly (e.g. no further sign-ins or sensitive-data access tied to that account after withdrawal)?

3. On the completion-time question the DPA is asking about — can we also show whether requests touching account security data (sessions, API/service credentials, access logs) take longer to close than others? That's often where delays hide.

Happy to compare notes before you send anything to data engineering.
