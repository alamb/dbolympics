Good to be working with you. I'm still building my mental map of the business, so I'd rather ask a few broad questions well than a lot of narrow ones badly. Given the slot budget, here's what I'd prioritize this week, roughly in order:

1. **How did we actually do in 2025, and where is the money coming from?** I want to see our own revenue split between the subscription/plan fees and the payment-processing fees, over the year, and ideally how that's trending month to month. This is the backbone of the annual accounts narrative and will shape how I talk about 2025 to the board.

2. **How concentrated is our business in a few large merchants, and how healthy are they?** I keep hearing "a handful of large merchants drive most of the volume" — I'd like to understand who they are, how much of our gross volume and revenue they represent, and whether that concentration is growing or shrinking. This matters for risk assessment going into the budget.

3. **What do refunds and returns look like across the merchant base?** I want a sense of refund rates — overall and whether certain verticals or merchants are outliers — since this affects both our net revenue and how much cash we actually end up paying out.

4. **How exposed are we to currency movements?** Merchants bill in their own currencies, and shoppers come from ~40 countries. I'd like a picture of how our volume and revenue break down by currency, so I can gauge our FX exposure for the 2026 budget and think about whether we need any hedging conversation.

5. **Are our B2B merchants paying us and their own customers on time?** I know a few of our merchants are wholesalers running on B2B terms. I'd like to understand how their receivables look — how much is outstanding and how overdue it tends to get — since this could be a real cash-flow risk if it's worse than I'd expect.

For this week, let's focus on #1 and #2 — those are the two I most need before I can speak credibly about 2025 and the 2026 budget. If there's room, #3 would be next; #4 and #5 can wait for a follow-up week if slots are tight.

Let me know if any of these need me to narrow scope further before you brief the data engineers.
